About Courage

In short terms you can definitely say that Courage is one of the bestest doggo's in the world.
But if you truly want to know more about him then read further!
Courage is a pink dog who lives in a house with his sweet Muriel and nasty Eustace.
His courage is tested every day by the many monsters and aliens that come to his house.

The creation of Courage

Courage the Cowardly Dog was created by John R. Dilworth and produced by Stretch Films.
The show was first aired on November 12, 1999 and ended on November 22, 2002.
The show has a total of 4 seasons and 52 episodes.
